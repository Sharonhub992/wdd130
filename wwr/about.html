Our History

Founded in 2010, our rafting company started with just two guides and a passion for the river. Over the years, we have safely guided thousands of thrill-seekers through the most exhilarating rapids in the region.

We pride ourselves on our certified safety standards, eco-friendly footprint, and creating unforgettable memories for families and extreme sports enthusiasts alike.
